# ADIS16470 Model

## Role

The ADIS16470 supplies three-axis angular rate and low-g specific force for ESKF
propagation. It has a dedicated SPI bus in the model.

## Sampling and timing

- Internal sample rate: 2000 Hz
- `DEC_RATE`: 0 through 1999
- Output rate: `2000 / (DEC_RATE + 1)` samples per second
- Default: `DEC_RATE=3`, or 500 Hz
- Data-ready timestamp: final 2000 Hz sample in each averaging group
- Arrival timestamp: data-ready plus one 176-bit transfer at configured SPI rate
- Maximum modeled SPI clock: 1 MHz

Each output is the arithmetic mean of `DEC_RATE + 1` noisy internal samples.

## Measurement chain

For every 2000 Hz truth sample, the model:

1. Reconstructs ideal body specific force and reads body angular rate.
2. Rotates body values into sensor axes with `sensor_to_body.T`.
3. Advances accelerometer and gyro bias random walks.
4. Applies per-axis scale error and the small-angle misalignment matrix
   `I + skew(misalignment_rad)`.
5. Adds independent Gaussian white noise.
6. Averages the configured decimation group.
7. Quantizes, clips, builds the burst response, and adds timing metadata.

Independent random state is derived from `[run_seed, ADIS16470 sensor ID]`.

## Raw scaling

| Channel | Scale | Modeled range | Encoding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acceleration | 800 LSB/g | plus or minus 40 g | Signed 16-bit word, clipped at plus or minus 32,000 counts |
| Angular rate | 10 LSB/(deg/s) | plus or minus 2000 deg/s | Signed 16-bit word, clipped at plus or minus 20,000 counts |
| Temperature | 10 LSB/deg C | Signed word | Constant configured temperature |
| Data counter | 1 count/sample | 0 through 65,535 | Unsigned wrapping word |

Decoded acceleration is returned in m/s^2 and angular rate in rad/s. A clipped
channel sets `SATURATED` on the logical event.

## Burst transaction

The exact transaction is 22 bytes, or 176 bits, in big-endian 16-bit words:

| Word | Field |
| ---: | --- |
| 0 | Burst command `0x6800` |
| 1 | `DIAG_STAT` |
| 2 to 4 | X, Y, Z gyro |
| 5 to 7 | X, Y, Z accelerometer |
| 8 | Temperature |
| 9 | Data counter |
| 10 | Checksum |

The `MeasurementEvent.payload` omits the command and contains the final ten
response words, for 20 bytes. The binary replay includes all eleven words.

The checksum is the unsigned sum of the high and low bytes of the nine words
from `DIAG_STAT` through data counter, reduced modulo 65,536.

## Fault injection

`FaultSchedule` supports deterministic output-index sets for:

- checksum corruption
- diagnostic status error
- duplicate data counter
- skipped data counter
- packet loss

Diagnostic and checksum faults clear `VALID`. Packet loss omits the event while
the internal counter and sequence continue advancing.

## Estimator handling

The estimator independently reparses the burst and rejects malformed payloads,
bad checksums, and nonzero diagnostic status. It tracks both logical sequence
continuity and the 16-bit device data counter. A valid sample supplies gyro and,
unless high-g mode is active, acceleration propagation.

## Calibration status

Default bias, bias random walk, scale error, misalignment, and mounting values
are zero or identity placeholders. Replace them with bench estimates rather than
interpreting datasheet maximum limits as Gaussian standard deviations.
